About Bharat Seeds

1993 Est.

Trusted Seeds for Better Harvest

Since 1993, Bharat Seeds has been serving the farming community with trust and dedication. Founded in Patiala, Punjab, by a family deeply connected to agriculture, the journey started with a small-scale seed business built on generations of farming knowledge and experience.

By staying closely connected with farmers over the years, we understood the changing needs of modern agriculture and expanded Bharat Seeds into a farmer-oriented company focused on providing quality seeds, farming products, and reliable agricultural solutions.
